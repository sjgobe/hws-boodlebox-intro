BoodleBox at HWS

Advancing AI literacy through intentional campus conversation

What the Discussion Was

Across HWS, conversations about generative AI focused on a central question: how do we respond thoughtfully to a technology that is already reshaping teaching, learning, and academic work?

The discussion surfaced several shared concerns:

  • Critical thinking: ensuring AI supports student learning rather than replacing it.
  • Transparency: creating open conversation about AI use instead of hidden or unclear use.
  • Privacy and security: protecting student work, research, and institutional data.
  • Equity: making sure access to AI tools does not depend on who can pay for them.
  • Assessment: rethinking how learning is demonstrated in an AI-enabled environment.
  • Intentionality: using AI in ways that align with the values and goals of the HWS community.

Why BoodleBox Answers Those Concerns

BoodleBox was selected as a response to those concerns because it offers a more intentional, educational, and institutionally grounded way to engage with AI.

  • Supports teaching and learning: BoodleBox can be used as a thinking partner, helping faculty and students engage critically rather than passively.
  • Encourages transparency: shared conversations and visible prompting make AI use easier to discuss openly in academic settings.
  • Protects privacy: the platform is designed to better safeguard user data and institutional work.
  • Promotes equity: students and faculty can access multiple AI models in one place without needing separate paid subscriptions.
  • Fits academic goals: BoodleBox helps HWS focus on AI literacy, responsible use, and community values instead of simple tool adoption.

At HWS, the goal is not simply to use AI more often, but to use it more thoughtfully.
