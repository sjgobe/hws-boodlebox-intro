AI Literacy Forward

What we want our community to practice.

AI literacy is not about memorizing one tool. It is about developing habits of inquiry, skepticism, creativity, and ethical judgment.

Understand how large language models work

Learn what these systems are good at, where they fail, and why they can produce confident but inaccurate responses.

Practice better prompting

Treat prompting as a form of communication: provide context, define goals, ask for reasoning, and revise based on results.

Evaluate output critically

Check claims, compare models, ask for sources when appropriate, and use human expertise to judge the quality of AI-generated work.

Use AI transparently and ethically

Discuss when AI use is appropriate, how it should be acknowledged, and how it connects to course or workplace expectations.

Responsible Adoption

We are not ignoring the hard questions.

BoodleBox helps HWS engage directly with concerns that matter: overuse, academic integrity, privacy, cost, environmental impact, and the changing nature of assessment.

🔒

Privacy of Data

Use a platform designed for institutional contexts rather than asking users to rely on public consumer tools.

🌱

Environmental Awareness

Build habits that encourage intentional, purposeful use of AI rather than casual, high-volume experimentation without reflection.

⚖️

Cost and Access

Support more equitable access so AI literacy does not depend on who can afford individual subscriptions.
